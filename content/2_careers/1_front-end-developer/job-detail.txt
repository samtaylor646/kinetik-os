Title: Front End Administrator

----

Description:

**Full job description**

**Job Summary**
We are looking for a Front-End Administrator to manage, optimize, and maintain the front-end experience across our Shopify storefronts. This role sits at the intersection of eCommerce operations, web production, merchandising, analytics, and development. The ideal candidate is highly detail-oriented, comfortable working inside Shopify themes and admin tools, and able to turn business requests into clean, accurate, conversion-focused site updates.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Site administration: Manage day-to-day updates across Shopify storefronts, including product pages, collections, navigation, landing pages, promotions, banners, and content blocks.
- Theme and front-end updates: Make front-end updates using Shopify theme settings, Liquid, HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript where needed.
- Merchandising support: Ensure products, collections, pricing, promotions, subscriptions, and bundles are displayed accurately and consistently.
- Content publishing: Build and update landing pages, campaign pages, blog content, educational pages, and promotional assets based on approved briefs.
- Quality assurance: Test site updates across desktop, mobile, browsers, customer types, discounts, and user flows before publishing.
- App and integration support: Help manage front-end behavior for Shopify apps, including subscriptions, reviews, loyalty, search, product recommendations, analytics, and promotional tools.
- Analytics and tracking coordination: Partner with marketing and analytics teams to ensure campaign links, pixels, tags, GA4 events, and promotional tracking are implemented correctly.
- Cross-functional execution: Work with marketing, creative, eCommerce, operations, customer service, and development teams to launch site updates on time and with high accuracy.
- Documentation: Maintain clear documentation for recurring Shopify processes, publishing workflows, QA checklists, and site-update standards.

**Required Qualifications**

2+ years of experience managing or supporting Shopify or Shopify Plus storefronts.
Working knowledge of Shopify admin, themes, products, collections, discounts, metafields, and page templates.
Basic front-end development skills, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Shopify Liquid.
Strong attention to detail and ability to QA customer-facing web experiences.
Experience supporting eCommerce promotions, product launches, landing pages, and campaign execution.
Comfortable working with project management tools and structured intake workflows.
Ability to troubleshoot front-end issues and clearly communicate technical findings to non-technical teams.
Strong sense of ownership, urgency, and follow-through.

**Preferred Qualifications**

Experience with Klaviyo, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Recharge or subscription tools, reviews platforms, loyalty tools, and Shopify apps.
Familiarity with SEO best practices, structured content, schema, and page performance.
Experience working in DTC, health and wellness, supplements, CPG, or regulated industries.
Basic understanding of accessibility, responsive design, and conversion rate optimization.
Experience with GitHub or version-controlled theme workflows is a plus.
Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is equal parts Shopify operator, front-end problem solver, and detail-driven web producer. They are comfortable making hands-on updates, testing carefully, documenting repeatable processes, and helping teams move faster without sacrificing accuracy. They should enjoy working in a fast-paced eCommerce environment where product launches, promotions, and site improvements are happening continuously.
